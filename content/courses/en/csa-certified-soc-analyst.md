## Course Overview
This course covers the fundamentals of SOC operations: log management and correlation, SIEM understanding and operations, and incident detection, response, and investigation. It also builds the skills to follow SOC processes and work with senior analysts and CSIRT when needed.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand SOC operations fundamentals, including log management and correlation, SIEM operations, and incident detection, response, and investigation
- Understand SOC processes and develop the skills to work with senior analysts and CSIRT when escalation is required
- Prepare to pass the international CSA certification exam
