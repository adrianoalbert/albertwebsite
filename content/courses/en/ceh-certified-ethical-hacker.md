## Course Overview
A program that systematically teaches real-world attack tactics, threats, tools, and methods. It builds the ability to think from an attacker’s perspective as an ethical hacker (white hat) and apply that insight to effective defense.

## Learning Objectives
- Review security management and controls, and understand ethical hacking ethics
- Learn how to operate attack tools across layers, understand attack scenarios, and practice evasion techniques
- Understand cryptography and the attacker’s perspective across environments, including the cloud
- Prepare to pass the international CEH certification exam
